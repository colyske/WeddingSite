How it works

From first draft to shared link in four steps

Start with the essentials and add more whenever you are ready. Nothing is public until you publish.

  1. Sign in

    Use Google or email. Your wedding stays a private draft while you build it.

  2. Add the essentials

    Your names, date and time zone, each venue with its address, and the colour palette that styles your whole site.

  3. Choose what guests can do

    RSVP and meals, the programme, song requests, a gift registry, fundraising campaigns, sponsorship or VIP seating. Use only what suits your day.

  4. Review, publish, share

    Check the publication quote, publish, then share your-names.joinourwedding.site by WhatsApp, SMS or email.

How long setup takes depends on how much you add. You can keep editing details from your dashboard after you publish.

Good to know

Questions couples ask

What does it cost?

Creating and editing a draft is free. Before you publish, you see a publication quote: it accounts for the first-wedding free allowance, and weddings more than 18 months away carry a separate fee. When guests pay, the platform fee is 8% of verified payments with no fixed per-contribution fee; provider transaction fees are deducted separately. See the terms for details.

How do guests pay, and when does a payment count?

The methods shown depend on your wedding's currency and the providers we support, for example M-Pesa for Kenyan-shilling weddings where enabled. A gift, bid or paid extra counts only after the payment provider confirms it to our server; a "request sent" message is not treated as payment.

How do we receive the money?

You request a payout of settled funds (after fees, refunds and holds) from your dashboard. Payouts require payout details for both partners and are reviewed by our team before money is transferred.

Do guests need an account?

No. Guests RSVP and look up their pass and seat with the phone number they were invited with. The guest site can also be switched between English and Swahili.

Who can see our wedding?

Drafts and unpublished sites are never listed. Once published, your site is public at its link; guests see only their own pass and seat, and contributor names appear only when the guest opts in.

Can we try it before creating our own?

Yes. The sample wedding shows the full guest experience. It is a showcase only, so it does not accept payments, RSVPs or messages.
