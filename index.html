Good to know

Questions couples ask

What does it cost?

Free to start: creating and editing a draft costs nothing. Conditional fees apply to some features, such as publishing in some cases and guest payments; you see what applies in your account before you publish, and guests see their total before they pay. See the terms for full details.

Can you guarantee our budget will be met?

No platform can promise how much guests will give. We make giving simple, show clear goals and progress, and keep an accurate record of every confirmed contribution, so you can plan with real numbers.

How do guests pay, and when does a payment count?

The methods shown depend on your wedding's currency and the providers we support, for example M-Pesa for Kenyan-shilling weddings where enabled. A gift, bid or paid extra counts only after the payment provider confirms it to our server; a "request sent" message is not treated as payment.

How do we receive the money?

You request a payout of settled funds (after fees, refunds and holds) from your dashboard. Payouts require payout details for both partners and are reviewed by our team before money is transferred.

Do guests need an account?

No. Guests RSVP and look up their pass and seat with the phone number they were invited with. The guest site can also be switched between English and Swahili.

Who can see our wedding?

Drafts and unpublished sites are never listed. Once published, your site is public at its link; guests see only their own pass and seat, and contributor names appear only when the guest opts in.

Can we try it before creating our own?

Yes. The sample wedding shows the full guest experience. It is a demonstration only, so it does not accept payments, RSVPs or messages. From scratch uses its reusable setup with generic editable prompts. Or browse published weddings and use an eligible public setup in a new private draft without copying personal content.
