Quick answers

Common questions

Is it free to start?

Yes! Creating and editing your wedding draft is free. When guests contribute, a small platform maintenance fee applies (around 4–8% depending on setup) and you see your numbers clearly on your dashboard. Full details are on our Terms and Privacy pages.

How do guests pay, and when does it count?

Guests can pay using supported methods for your currency (such as M-Pesa for Kenyan shillings and credit cards where enabled). A contribution counts as soon as the payment provider confirms it.

How do we get our money?

Once funds clear, you just request a payout from your dashboard to the couple's payout details after quick verification, and the funds are sent to you ASAP. We do not hold the money, it is yours.

Do guests need to download an app or create an account?

No! Guests just tap your link, view the schedule, RSVP, check their seat or QR entry pass, and contribute. It is in English and Swahili.

Can we test it before sharing with guests?

Yes. Check out the sample wedding first, and build your own page in private draft mode until you are ready to publish.
