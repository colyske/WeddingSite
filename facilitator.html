Important: current referral status

A 2% setting can calculate estimates; it doesn’t enable payouts.

The Owner-controlled policy rate and the payable balance are different. If the rate is set to 2% and enabled, the platform can calculate a HELD estimate for eligible new verified live contributions. The current Client Referrals page still reports payable as zero: the backend has no enabled payout route, and settlement, full cost records and other platform commitments are not yet verified. This is why a 2% rate can coexist with zero payable.

The rate is prospective: it applies to new eligible contributions, not old ones; weddings created before the first activation are ineligible. Any eventual commission would be capped by available platform revenue, not deducted from the couple’s net. Never promise a payout date or amount, and never ask a couple to pay you or send contributions through you.

A consent-first process

From introduction to a couple’s own wedding page

Follow these steps in order. The current website flow is referral-based: the couple—not the facilitator—creates and owns the page.

  1. 01

    Opt in and copy your personal link

    Sign in, open Client Referrals from your dashboard, review and accept the current terms, then copy your unique referral link. Opt-in is recorded and can be withdrawn.

  2. 02

    Show the couple what the site does

    Walk through the Showcase Wedding together. It is a fictional, read-only sample; it does not accept payments, RSVPs or messages.

  3. 03

    Ask permission before sharing your link

    Explain that referral attribution is optional, the couple will create their own account, and any applicable platform charges are shown in the site before publishing or paying. Keep their written reply or other clear record of consent.

  4. 04

    Send the link by WhatsApp or SMS

    Use a message the couple can keep. Invite them to open the link, sign in themselves and review the current terms. Never request their password, sign-in code or recovery code.

  5. 05

    Guide, don’t take over

    Talk them through names, date, venue, schedule, campaign goals and optional features. The couple enters and checks their own details, payout number and any requested verification, then chooses when to publish.

  6. 06

    Respect their attribution choice

    When creating the wedding, the couple may accept or decline referral attribution. The first valid referral capture wins; accepted attribution is immutable. A referral never grants you access to their wedding.

  7. 07

    Check your referral summary

    Return to Client Referrals for the permitted aggregate summary. The current interface labels estimates HELD and payable zero; it does not offer facilitator withdrawals or transfers.

A message you can adapt

Ask clearly. Keep the reply.

Get permission before sending your referral link. This wording reflects the current couple-led setup process; do not reuse older wording that says you will create or publish the page for them.

I’d like to send you my Join Our Wedding referral link. You’ll sign in and create and manage your own wedding page; I won’t access your account or handle contributions. Please review any fees shown on the site before publishing or paying. Referral attribution is optional and you can decline it when creating your wedding. May I send you the link?

Save their written reply. Keep it private and share it only if needed to resolve a referral question.

Set honest expectations

What to say—and what to avoid

Good to say

  • “You create and manage your own wedding page.”
  • “Creating a draft is free; check the site for any fees before publishing or paying.”
  • “You can review and decline referral attribution.”
  • “Your dashboard shows confirmed contributions and any applicable deductions.”
  • “The showcase is a sample; it doesn’t accept payments or RSVPs.”

Never promise

  • “There are no charges at all.”
  • “You’ll receive every shilling” or a guaranteed fundraising result.
  • “I’ll collect the contributions” or “send the money to me.”
  • A 2% setting as a guaranteed payout, a payout date or guaranteed facilitator earnings.
  • To create a page without consent, use your own payout details or recruit another facilitator for a cut.

Self-referrals are not allowed. Do not refer a wedding you own, a family member’s wedding or a wedding you personally benefit from. This is a one-level referral program; do not recruit facilitators or promise a share of anyone else’s commission.
