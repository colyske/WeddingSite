A hands-on, consent-first process

The facilitator workflow

  1. 01

    Prepare once

    Get started in just a moment: sign in once, opt in to Client Referrals and copy your unique setup link. Use that same link for every couple you assist. To qualify, be 18 or older, have valid ID and an M-Pesa number in your own name. There is no joining fee or target.

  2. 02

    Get the couple’s consent in person

    Be physically with the couple and show them the sample wedding. Ask them to send the consent message below and keep their reply before starting setup.

    I'll set up your wedding website on Join Our Wedding site for you. Setup is free. When you raise money through the site, the platform charges a percentage of what's raised (around 4-8% for maintenance); you keep the rest (92-96%). You'll see every deduction on your dashboard. Can I go ahead?

  3. 03

    Send the setup link and sign in together

    After they agree, send this message by WhatsApp or SMS so there is a timestamped record. Replace the placeholder with your setup link, then stay with them while they sign in on their own phone or laptop.

    Here's the link to set up your wedding site: [your referral link]. Click it, sign in, and I'll help you fill everything in.

  4. 04

    Complete the wedding on their device together

    Help enter the couple’s names, date, story, theme, venue, full schedule, fundraising goals, gifts, song requests, sponsors, VIP seats and RSVP settings. The couple reviews each detail and enters their own payout and identity information. Never use your own payout number or ask for their password or sign-in code.

  5. 05

    Review and publish

    Check every detail and fee with the couple. They approve the finished page and choose when to publish.

  6. 06

    Help them share and get the most from the site

    Show how to share the link in family, church or mosque, neighborhood and committee groups. Demonstrate campaign progress, gifts, song bidding, sponsorship and RSVPs, and stay available for questions and updates. Never handle contributions, refer a wedding you benefit from, or recruit other facilitators for a cut.

  7. 07

    Track your commission

    Check Client Referrals for eligible contribution totals and commission by currency.
