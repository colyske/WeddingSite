Trust comes first

4 golden rules to keep your account safe

We want you to make great money, so please follow these simple rules:

  • Always get a “Yes” first: Never set up a page behind a couple's back.
  • Never touch their money. Always put the couple's M-Pesa number as the payout account. Never tell guests to send contributions to your personal phone.
  • Be honest about fees: Let them know that setup is free, and the platform only takes a small percentage (4–8%) from donations to run the system.
  • No self-referrals or pyramid schemes: You cannot set up your own wedding to earn commission, and you cannot recruit other facilitators to take a cut of their pay.
