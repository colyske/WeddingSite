A consent-first process

From introduction to a couple’s own wedding page

Follow these steps in order. The current website flow is referral-based: the couple—not the facilitator—creates and owns the page.

  1. 01

    Opt in and prepare your setup link

    Sign in to Join Our Wedding, open Client Referrals from your dashboard, accept the current terms and copy your unique setup link. The couple does not need to manage this step.

  2. 02

    Meet the couple, get consent and begin together

    Be physically with the couple. Show them the Showcase Wedding, then ask them to send the consent message below and keep their reply. With their agreement, open the setup link on one of the couple’s phones or laptops and start the site together.

  3. 03

    Set up the wedding on the couple’s device

    Work together on the couple’s own phone or laptop. Help enter their names, date, venue, schedule, goals, sponsors and optional features. The couple provides and confirms their own payout and identity details. Never use your own payout details or ask for their password or sign-in code.

  4. 04

    Review the page and publish

    Read every detail back with the couple, check their goals and event information, and let them approve the finished page before it is published.

  5. 05

    Show them how to share and use the features

    Help share the wedding site with family, faith communities and friends. Demonstrate campaign progress, gifts, song bidding, sponsorship and RSVP features that suit their celebration.

  6. 06

    Track your referral commission

    Check Client Referrals for your eligible contribution totals and commission. The Platform pays after funds are cleared.

I'll set up your wedding website on Join Our Wedding site for you. Setup is free. When you raise money through the site, the platform charges a percentage of what's raised (around 4-8% for maintenance); you keep the rest (92-96%). You'll see every deduction on your dashboard. Can I go ahead?

Set honest expectations

What to say—and what to avoid

Good to say

  • “You create and manage your own wedding page.”
  • “Creating a draft is free; check the site for any fees before publishing or paying.”
  • “You can review and decline referral attribution.”
  • “Your dashboard shows confirmed contributions and any applicable deductions.”
  • “The showcase is a sample; it doesn’t accept payments or RSVPs.”

Never promise

  • “There are no charges at all.”
  • “You’ll receive every shilling” or a guaranteed fundraising result.
  • “I’ll collect the contributions” or “send the money to me.”
  • A guaranteed fundraising target or an amount before eligible contributions are verified.
  • To create a page without consent, use your own payout details or recruit another facilitator for a cut.

Self-referrals are not allowed. Do not refer a wedding you own, a family member’s wedding or a wedding you personally benefit from. This is a one-level referral program; do not recruit facilitators or promise a share of anyone else’s commission.
