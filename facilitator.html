First, what is Join Our Wedding?

Your free wedding website

Planning a wedding is expensive, and chasing contributions in WhatsApp groups is a headache. Join Our Wedding gives couples a free wedding website to share with friends, family and workmates.

01

All wedding info in one place

Dates, times, church and venue directions, dress colours, pre-wedding parties, rehearsals and harambee meeting details.

02

Song bidding

Guests can pay to request songs and bid to move a song up the DJ’s list.

03

Business & VIP sponsors

Local businesses and other sponsors can pay to show their adverts on the wedding website. Guests can also pay for VIP seats.

04

Live contribution bar

Everyone can see the fundraising goal and watch progress as contributions come in.

Your role, made simple

So, what is your job as a Facilitator?

Think about every town in Kenya: wedding committees meet in local hotels, restaurants and halls to plan weddings and raise funds. Your job is to meet couples, show them the website and help them get started.

  1. 01

    Get your special link

    Go to JoinOurWedding.site and sign in. Open your Dashboard, tap Client Referrals, then Opt In and copy your personal link. Save it somewhere easy to find on your phone.

    Sign in & get your free facilitator link →
  2. 02

    Visit a wedding meeting & show them the demo

    Meet the couple or committee at a wedding meeting, or talk to friends who are planning a wedding. Open the wedding example on your phone and let them scroll through it.

    See the wedding example →
  3. 03

    Ask permission and send your link on WhatsApp

    Never create a website for someone without permission. Send them this message while you are together and wait for their “Yes” before you start.

    Hi! I'd love to help you set up your free wedding website on Join Our Wedding. Setup is free. We show you the price of any paid feature before you choose it. You'll be in charge of your wedding page and M-Pesa details. Would you like us to set it up now? If yes, tap this link and I'll help you fill in your wedding details: [Paste your referral link]

  4. 04

    Set it up on their phone

    After they agree, help them sign in on their own phone or laptop and fill in their wedding details. They enter their own M-Pesa number—never yours.

    Help them add their names, wedding date and venue, schedule, harambee details, fundraising goal, VIP seats and sponsor packages.

  5. 05

    Teach them how to share

    Show them how to share their link in family, church, mosque, workplace and committee WhatsApp groups. Point out the contribution progress, song requests and VIP seating. Referral estimates remain held and unpayable through the platform.

Trust comes first

4 golden rules to keep your account safe

  • Always get a “Yes” first. Never set up a page without the couple’s permission.
  • Never touch their money. The couple enters their own M-Pesa details. Never ask guests to send contributions to your phone.
  • Be honest about fees. Setup is free. Show the couple the price of any paid feature before they choose it. Never promise “0% fees” or that they will definitely raise a certain amount.
  • No self-referrals or pyramid schemes. Do not refer your own wedding or recruit other helpers for a cut of their commission.
