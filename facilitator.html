Your role, made simple

Five steps to helping a couple

You are a friendly guide, not the owner of their account. Be physically with the couple and let them make the decisions.

  1. 01

    Get your link

    Sign in, opt in to Client Referrals and copy your unique link. Use the same link for every couple you assist.

    Go to Client Referrals →
  2. 02

    Show the sample

    The public sample is being updated. For now, use the illustration on this page to explain the wedding website and its features.

    Check the sample wedding →
  3. 03

    Ask for their written consent

    Send this by WhatsApp or SMS, discuss the applicable fees shown in the quotes, and ask them to reply “Yes” before starting setup. Keep their reply as a record.

    I'll help you set up your wedding website on Join Our Wedding. Creating and editing your draft is free. Some publication or payment features may have fees; we'll check the exact quotes together before you decide. If you use my link, you can choose whether to accept referral attribution. Can I go ahead?

  4. 04

    Send your link & help them set up

    Once they agree, send your link by WhatsApp or SMS. Stay with them as they sign in on their own phone or laptop and enter their details. They review the information and fees, choose whether to accept referral attribution, and decide when to publish.

    Here's the link to set up your wedding site: [your referral link]. Click it, sign in, and I'll help you fill everything in.

  5. 05

    Help them share

    Show them how to share their wedding link with family, friends and committee groups. Stay available for questions. Check Client Referrals for eligible referral activity; commissions are paid after eligible funds clear to your verified M-Pesa details.

Trust comes first

The golden rules

  • Get consent. Explain the referral and keep the couple’s written agreement. They can decline attribution.
  • Let the couple stay in control. They enter their own identity and payout details. Never ask for a password or sign-in code, use your own payout number, or collect contributions.
  • Be open about fees. Review the applicable publication, payment and withdrawal quotes with the couple. Do not promise a fixed deduction or payout date.
  • Keep it fair. Do not refer a wedding you benefit from or recruit facilitators for a cut. This is not a multilevel scheme.
