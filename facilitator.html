First, what is Join Our Wedding?

Planning a wedding is expensive, and chasing contributions in WhatsApp groups is a headache. Join Our Wedding gives couples a wedding website they can start for free and share with friends, family and workmates.

01

All wedding info in one place

Dates, times, church and venue directions, dress code colours, pre-wedding parties, rehearsals and harambee meeting details.

02

03

Business & VIP sponsors

VIP seating

  3. 03

    Ask permission and send your link on WhatsApp (Very Important!)

    Never create a website for someone without their permission. While sitting with them, send them this WhatsApp message so you have proof they agreed, and wait for their “Yes” before you start.

    Hi! I'd love to help you set up your wedding website on Join Our Wedding. Starting a draft is free. When guests contribute money through the site, platform fees are shown in the payment quote. You'll also see charges before choosing paid features. You'll be in charge of your wedding page and M-Pesa details. Should we set it up now? If yes, tap this link to sign in and I'll help you fill in your wedding details: [Paste your referral link]

  4. 04

    Set it up on their phone

    After they agree, help them sign in on their own phone or laptop and fill in their wedding details. They enter their own M-Pesa number—never yours.

    Help them add their names, wedding date and venue, schedule, harambee details, fundraising goal, VIP seats and sponsor packages.

  5. 05

    Teach them how to share it

    Don't just set it up and walk away! Show them how to share their link in family, church, mosque, temple, workplace and committee WhatsApp groups. Remind them to tell local businesses about sponsor slots, and show them how guests can bid on songs for the DJ.

Trust comes first

4 golden rules to keep your account safe

  • Always get a “Yes” first. Never set up a page behind a couple’s back.
  • Never touch their money. Always put the couple's M-Pesa number as the payout account. Never tell guests to send contributions to your personal phone.
  • Be honest about fees. Starting a draft is free, and contribution fees are shown in the payment quote. Show the couple any charges before they choose a paid feature. Never promise “0% fees” or that they will definitely raise a certain amount.
  • No self-referrals or pyramid schemes. Do not refer your own wedding or recruit other helpers for a cut of their commission.
