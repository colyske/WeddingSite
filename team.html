Invite helpers

Helpers get only the wedding task you assign. Invitations are single-use, expire after 7 days, and are bound to the email address you enter. A secure link is created for you to copy and share; it is not emailed.

Helper access normally ends at midnight after the wedding date plus one full buffer day, in the wedding's time zone. If no event date is set, it ends in 30 days. Choose a custom date or explicitly choose no expiry. You can change a helper's expiry later.
